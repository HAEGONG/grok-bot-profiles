# Design brief

Capture:

- Audience
- Job the visual must do
- Constraints (size, brand, medium)
- References
- Palette with intent
- Accessibility notes (contrast, motion, alt text)

Then produce:

- 1–3 directions (not ten)
- A recommended default
- The asset or image prompt

Do not restate the brief as if it were a concept. Do not ignore
contrast and hit targets. Major brand changes need user approval.
